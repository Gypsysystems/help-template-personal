Using Map Toolbar

The Map Toolbar provides tools for searching locations, drawing areas, managing shapes, and visualizing movement.

Searching for a Location/Area

You can search for locations, landmarks, points of interest, or the areas you draw on map and locate them on the map.

To search for a location, do the following:

  1. Log in to Synergy.
  2. From the Open Workspace drop-down, select the relevant workspace. All available workspace components are displayed.
  3. Mouse over Tools and click Map Explorer. The Map Explorer component is displayed.
  4. On the Map, click the Search icon. A pop-up is displayed.
  5. In the search box, enter a location. The map displays and centers on the selected location.

Drawing Areas

You can draw geographic areas on the map to visually define regions and use them for filtering and analysis.

To draw areas, do the following:

  1. On the Map explorer, in the Map toolbar, select the Polygon Areas icon. The Polygon Areas pop-up is displayed.

  2. In the pop-up, you can select one of the following:

    • Circle

    • Rectangle

    • Polygon

  3. Click on the map to draw the selected shape. The drawn areas are saved in the Polygon Areas pop-up under the Areas section.
    By default the areas are saved as No area name.

  4. Click the name to modify it. For example, rename No area name to Area 1.

  5. After adding the shape to the Areas section, from the vertical 3 dots menu, you can do the following:

    • To zoom and view the center of the selected shape, click Center view to polygons.

    • To save the selected area and make it visible with other members, do the following:

      1. Click Save area. The Save area pop-up is displayed.
      2. Enter a name for the area and click Save. The selected area is saved and displayed under Shared Areas section.
    • To add an area to a group, do the following:
      1. Click Save to group area. The Save to group area pop-up is displayed.
        • To add the area to an existing group, from the Add to an existing area drop-down, select a relevant group name.
        • To add it to a new group, in the New area name field, enter a name for the new group.
      2. Click Submit. The area is saved within a group folder.
    • To remove an area from the map, click Delete.

  1. To view all the saved areas, click Saved areas.

  2. To remove a Shared Area, from the vertical three dots menu, click Remove area from map.

  3. To view or hide the polygons or the labels on the map, click the Settings icon and disable or enable the respective toggle icons.

Measuring Distance

You can measure the distance between locations on the map.

To measure distance, do the following:

  1. Select Measure Distance from the Polygon Areas toolbar.

  2. Click two or more points on the map to measure the distance between them. The measured distance is displayed on the map.

Performing Area Set Operations

Map Explorer supports set‑based operations on drawn areas to enable advanced spatial analysis.

In the Polygon Areas pop-up, the following set operations are available when you select two or more areas on the map:

Set Operations

Description
Union

Union enables you to combine multiple areas into a single area.

Select two or more areas and click Union. A new area is created under the Areas section.
Difference

Difference enables you to subtract one area from another.

Select two areas that overlap and click Difference. A new area is created under the Areas section.
Intersection

Intersection enables you to find the common overlapping portion between areas.

Select two areas that overlap and click Intersection. A new area is created under the Areas section.
Split

Split enables you to divide a complex area into smaller parts.

Select one or more areas that contain multiple parts and click Split. A new area is created under the Areas section.
Note:

  • When performing set operations on two or more areas, the selected areas are hidden and new areas are created as an output under the Areas section.

  • By default, the system hides the original areas, but you can choose to show or hide them as needed.

Managing Points

Point Management enables you to create and manage pinned geographic locations on the map.

To create geo points, do the following:

  1. Right-click the required location on the map and click Pin location. A pop-up is displayed.

  2. In the Create point text box, enter a name. For example, Dubai Sports Club.

  3. Click Save. The point is pinned and highlighted on the map.

    Note:

    • To view the saved points, in the Map toolbar, click the Saved Points icon.

    • Pinned points can be hidden, edited, or deleted as required.

Using Movement Playback

Movement Playback enables you to visualize the movement of points or paths over time using date and time information associated with geo‑coordinated records.

Movement Playback is useful for analyzing trends such as travel patterns, movement history, and time‑based location changes.

To use movement playback, do the following:

Note: Ensure that a Point layer or Path layer with a date‑time field is added to the map.

  1. On the Map explorer, in the Map toolbar, click the Movement Playback icon. The playback panel is displayed at the bottom of the map.

  2. In the playback panel, review the following elements:

    • Current date and time: Displays the current playback timestamp.

    • Time range selector: Displays the start and end time for playback.

    • Stops by: Defines how playback steps are calculated (for example, by count).

    • Stops: Specifies the number of playback intervals.

      Note: For smoother playback animations, adjust the Stops value based on the data volume.

  3. Use the playback controls to do the following:

    • Play: Start the movement playback.

    • Pause: Pause the playback.

    • Step forward or backward: Navigate through movement step‑by‑step.

    As playback progresses, points or paths are animated on the map based on the selected time range and configuration.
